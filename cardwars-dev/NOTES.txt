CARD WARS  (cardwars.html)
A fan-made, offline, single-file tribute to the Adventure Time episode "Card Wars" (S4E14). Not affiliated
with Cartoon Network. Vanilla JS + WebGL (3D table) + Canvas 2D (labels, art) + DOM/CSS (UI). No libraries,
fonts, images, audio files or network requests: every model, texture and sound is generated in code.

ASSUMPTIONS / DECISIONS
- The director's note wins: the game plays like Card Wars as seen in the show. Where the episode is silent,
  gaps are filled from the official Card Wars rules (2014). The earlier "Glowboard" theme was dropped.
- The look: Finn and Jake's table in the Tree Fort with the props from the episode (Total Soda bottle,
  COOL GUY and DWEEB cups, Crunch chips, card box). Physical cards lie on the table edge; tiny hologram
  troops stand on a huge battlefield (about 4x the first 3D version in each direction) in front of big
  building holograms, and march across it to attack. The camera starts zoomed into your side: drag to scroll,
  pinch or the mouse wheel to zoom, and the map button (or M) shows the whole field. Finn's side glows blue, the
  opponent's orange; a stolen creature takes its new owner's colour. Models are simple low-poly cel-shaded
  shapes modelled on the episode's holograms.
- Episode rules used as written: "floop your land cards" makes your land and buildings appear; "keep those
  honeys hidden"; the first player starts; "discard a card and pick up a new one" at the start of a turn;
  you FLOOP a card (turn it left) to use its ability and ACTIVATE a creature (turn it right) to attack;
  "what'll you use to defend?" after the attacker calls battle; the loser is the Dweeb, the winner the
  Cool Guy. Show cards behave as in the episode (Silo of Truth, the Pig eating Cornfields, Husker Knights
  drawing energy from corn, Ancient Scholar studying Raise the Dead in the Schoolhouse, Cave of Solitude,
  Spirit Tower, Cerebral Bloodstorm, Field of Nightmares + Legion of Earlings, Volcano, Reclaim Landscape,
  Immortal Maize Walker, Summon Archer Dan, the Pig stuck in the mud, Field Reaper).
- Win condition: wipe out the other kingdom (every creature and building). A player whose side is empty at
  the end of any turn loses (both empty = draw). Kingdom landmarks start on the board. From round 13 the
  kingdoms crumble (overtime damage); at round 24 the judges compare kingdoms, so games always end.
- Assumed numbers (not stated in the show): 4 lanes; 2 Actions per turn; a card's cost is both its Actions
  and how many face-up landscapes of its type you need (official rule); hand refills to 5 after the discard;
  secret setup budget of 2 Actions (the second player gets 3 and one extra card, which keeps first-player
  wins under 54%); 20-card decks, max 2 copies (brief).
- Four extra landscapes (Useless Swamp, IcyLands, NiceLands, LavaFlats) and their characters fill out a
  six-faction game; their cards are inventions in the show's spirit. Card levels: L2 +1 DEF, L3 +1 ATK,
  L4 +1 DEF. Balance sims use level 1.
- The AI plans on Engine.redact(state): it never sees hidden hands, deck order or face-down buildings.
- If WebGL is unavailable the game shows a message instead of the board.

  /* AI */     ... /* END AI */      beam-search opponent using only redacted states

  /* ART */ /* AUDIO */ /* UI */ /* META */ /* TUTORIAL */ /* MAIN */
